MULTI-RAG DEMO — CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY
========================================

Last updated: 2026-08-10
Document type: policy
Tags: returns, shipping, support

1. Return window
----------------
Customers may return unused items within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be in original packaging with tags attached.
Final-sale and personalized items cannot be returned.

2. Refunds
----------
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days
after the warehouse receives and inspects the return.
Store credit is available immediately once the return is approved.

3. Exchanges
------------
Size or color exchanges are free for orders shipped within the contiguous US.
International exchanges are not supported; please return and reorder instead.

4. Shipping
-----------
Standard shipping: 3–5 business days.
Express shipping: 1–2 business days.
Orders placed after 2:00 PM local warehouse time ship the next business day.

5. Damaged or missing items
---------------------------
Report damage or missing items within 48 hours of delivery.
Email support@example.com with the order ID and photos of the package/item.
We will ship a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund.

6. Contact
----------
Support hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST.
Chat is available in the app; email responses usually arrive within 1 business day.
