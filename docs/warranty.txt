WARRANTY MANUAL

Effective 2026-01-01

1. Coverage window
   All Electronics-category products carry a 2-year limited warranty against
   manufacturing defects, starting on the delivery date. Apparel and Home
   products carry a 1-year limited warranty. Books, Grocery, and consumables
   are not warrantied.

2. What is covered
   - Component failures under normal use.
   - Battery capacity dropping below 70% of the rated value within the first
     12 months (Electronics only).
   - Peeling coatings, broken zippers, and stitching failures (Apparel only,
     within the first 90 days).

3. What is NOT covered
   - Accidental damage, drops, spills, submersion.
   - Cosmetic wear (scratches, fading) that does not affect function.
   - Damage caused by third-party accessories or unauthorised repairs.
   - Loss or theft (register the serial number with your account so you can
     transfer the warranty to a replacement device if needed).

4. How to file a warranty claim
   a. Locate the order number (or serial number for Electronics).
   b. Email support@example.com with the number, a description of the issue,
      and photos or a short video where relevant.
   c. Our support team will respond within 2 business days with either a
      remote fix, a replacement, or a repair depot address.
   d. Turnaround for repairs is typically 7-14 business days after we receive
      the device.

5. Extended protection plans
   Purchased separately at checkout. These extend the coverage window by an
   additional 12 or 24 months and add accidental-damage protection with a
   deductible ($30 for Apparel/Home items, $75 for Electronics).

6. Transferability
   Warranty coverage travels with the product, not the buyer, provided the
   original serial number is intact. Contact support to update the registered
   owner after a private sale or gift.
