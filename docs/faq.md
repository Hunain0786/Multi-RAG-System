# Customer FAQ

### How long does shipping take?

Domestic shipping is 2-5 business days for standard delivery and 1-2 business
days for expedited. International shipping is 7-14 business days and depends on
customs clearance in the destination country.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to 32 countries. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout for
most destinations and included in the price you pay. For a small number of
destinations (currently AE, SG, and JP) duties are collected by the carrier on
delivery.

### Can I change my address after ordering?

Only if the order has not yet been marked as **shipped**. Sign in, open the
order, and click "Edit address". Once the order is shipped the address is fixed
and you'll need to contact the carrier directly.

### How do I cancel an order?

You can cancel any order in the "pending" or "paid" states from your account.
Once the order is "shipped" it can no longer be cancelled — instead you can
refuse delivery or return the parcel under our return policy.

### What payment methods are supported?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), digital wallets (Apple Pay,
Google Pay, PayPal), bank transfer (select regions), and cash-on-delivery in
IN and NG. Cryptocurrency is not supported.

### Do you offer volume discounts?

For SME and Enterprise segment accounts, yes. Contact the sales team at
`sales@example.com` — discounts scale with order value and start at 3% for
orders above USD 2,000 and reach 15% for orders above USD 25,000.

### How do I track my order?

Once your order is marked as shipped you'll receive an email with a tracking
link. You can also see the tracking number in your account under the order
detail page.

### My package was marked as delivered but I didn't receive it — what do I do?

Check with neighbours and with any building concierge/reception first, since
carriers often leave parcels there. If you still can't locate the package,
contact us within 48 hours of the "delivered" scan and we'll open an
investigation with the carrier and issue a replacement or refund if warranted.

### Do you have a physical store?

No — we operate online only. However we have four regional distribution
centres (Bay Area, London, Bengaluru, Frankfurt) which handle order fulfilment
and returns.
