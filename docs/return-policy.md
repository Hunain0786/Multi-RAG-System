# Return Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-01. This document supersedes all prior versions._

## Standard return window

Most items purchased from our store are eligible for a **30-day return** starting
from the delivery date recorded in your account. To be eligible for a return the
item must be:

- unused and in the same condition you received it,
- in its original packaging,
- accompanied by the receipt or proof of purchase (order number is sufficient).

## Category-specific rules

- **Electronics.** Returned within 30 days if unopened, or within 15 days if
  opened but in working condition. Items with visible cosmetic damage or missing
  accessories are refused. Batteries and consumables (cables, chargers) sold
  separately are non-refundable once opened.
- **Apparel.** Returned within 30 days with all tags attached. Underwear,
  swimwear, and any final-sale items are non-returnable.
- **Grocery.** Non-returnable unless the item arrived damaged or spoiled. In
  that case contact support within 48 hours of delivery for a replacement.
- **Books.** Returned within 30 days if unread. A book is considered "read" if
  the spine is creased or pages are marked.

## Refunds

Once we receive your return and inspect it we will notify you by email. Approved
refunds are processed within **5 to 10 business days** back to the original
payment method. If you paid by card, the bank may take an additional 2-3
business days to reflect the refund on your statement.

Partial refunds may apply in these cases:

1. Items with obvious signs of use.
2. Missing parts (cables, remotes, manuals).
3. Items returned more than 30 days after delivery (case-by-case, at our
   discretion).

## Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged in shipping. Contact
support at `support@example.com` with your order number and clear photos.

## Non-returnable items

- Gift cards.
- Downloadable software.
- Personalized/engraved goods.
- Any final-sale item marked as such at checkout.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your account and open the order.
2. Click "Return items" and select the products you wish to send back.
3. Print the pre-paid return label.
4. Drop the parcel at the nearest carrier location within 14 days.
